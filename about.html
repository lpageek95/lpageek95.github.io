whoami

I'm Mario, a UNIX/OpenShift systems administrator based in Costa Rica, with more than ten years in infrastructure. Today I run Red Hat OpenShift clusters and the RHEL, Ubuntu and Oracle Linux fleet around them: upgrades, vulnerability remediation, DNS, firewalls, and the occasional late-night incident. Configuration lives in Git, and Ansible does the repetitive parts.

I came to platforms through networking and support. I spent years on the Juniper TAC working on SRX routing and security, then supported GPON and xDSL networks for service providers. At VMware I was a senior product support engineer for Tanzu, where I took Kubernetes cases from Fortune 500 customers, reproduced them in labs, and mentored newer engineers.

Lately I use AI tools like Claude and Gemini to make operations less repetitive: research, scripting, troubleshooting, and documentation. This site came out of that. It's where I keep the runbooks I've written, in the hope they save someone else an evening of debugging.
